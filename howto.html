STAMP AND STROLL

How to use Stamp and Stroll

A quick guide for visitors using the prototype app.

1

Sign up

Enter your phone number, select your country code, and verify with OTP.

2

Choose your path

Select either Walker or Buggy Tour based on how you want to explore the kampung.

3

Use the map

Walker users can tap map hotspots to learn about each themed stop and location.

4

Buggy queue and live trail

Buggy users choose a route, get a queue ticket, pay, then follow the live trail page.

5

Complete the photo hunt

Upload checkpoint photos to complete the activity and unlock the mascot reward.

6

Collect physical stamps

Stamps are collected physically on-site and combine into a take-home souvenir image.
